Anatomy-Routed ABBC-Affinity STU-Net Cascade (PENGWIN-Auto)

[Description] (max 1024 chars)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Pelvic and femoral fracture-fragment instance segmentation in CT.

The method is a serial two-stage STU-Net-B cascade. Stage A predicts sacrum,
left/right hipbone and femur, followed by the validated hybrid pelvic/femur
router. Stage B always selects the corresponding Sacrum, shared-Hip, or Femur
expert. Each expert starts from the TotalSegmentator-base V308 checkpoint,
freezes the encoder, and tunes its decoder and ABBC+9-affinity heads for three
epochs. Average-linkage agglomeration at T=0.75 converts affinities to instances
in the official 1-200 ranges. On a 68-patient/132-anatomy proxy, always-expert
reached IoU-F 0.8168, Dice 0.8556, HD95 6.687 mm, ASSD 1.852 mm and instance F1
0.9164. It improves overlap/surface metrics but adds 12 split errors, so it is
an independent leaderboard candidate rather than an automatic replacement.
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Job requires gpu type]
----------------------------------------------------------------
NVIDIA T4 Tensor Core GPU (16 GiB VRAM)

Stage A and each expert are loaded and released serially, so peak VRAM is the
maximum of one network rather than the sum of all experts.
