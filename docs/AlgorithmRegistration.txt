Anatomy-Routed Guarded-Seed Affinity-ABBC STU-Net (PENGWIN-Auto)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Description] (max 1024 chars)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Pelvic and femoral fracture-fragment instance segmentation in CT. A serial
two-stage STU-Net-B cascade predicts anatomy, routes pelvic/femur cases with a
validated hybrid random forest, and applies a Sacrum, shared-Hip, or Femur
expert producing four ABBC and nine 1/3/9-voxel affinity channels. The exact
v3.5 one-voxel affinity partition is the safe base. A binary ABBC Boundary
split is accepted only when both pieces contain at least 50 Core voxels, all
three affinity ranges support separation, and both pieces are at least 1,000
mm3. Post-split hard merging and Stage-1 fill are disabled; foreground support
is unchanged. All models and weights remain v3.5. On a frozen 68-patient/
132-anatomy local proxy, Dice changed 0.8557 to 0.8627, HD95 6.687 to 6.359 mm,
ASSD 1.852 to 1.766 mm, recall 0.9314 to 0.9377, F1 0.9164 to 0.9202, Merge
21 to 20, and Split 330 to 337. These are local proxy, not hidden-test, values.
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Job requires gpu type]
----------------------------------------------------------------
NVIDIA T4 Tensor Core GPU (16 GiB VRAM)

Stage A and each anatomy expert are loaded and released serially. Guarded-seed
refinement runs deterministically on CPU after each Stage-B anatomy crop.
