Guarded-Seed + Stage1-Fill Affinity-ABBC STU-Net (PENGWIN-Auto)

[Description] (max 1024 chars)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Pelvic and femoral fracture-fragment instance segmentation in CT. A serial
two-stage STU-Net-B cascade predicts anatomy, routes pelvic/femur cases with a
validated hybrid random forest, and applies a Sacrum, shared-Hip, or Femur
expert producing four ABBC and nine 1/3/9-voxel affinity channels. The exact
v3.5 one-voxel affinity partition is refined by guarded ABBC Boundary splits
requiring at least 50 Core voxels per side, agreement from all 1/3/9-voxel
affinity ranges, and final pieces of at least 1,000 mm3. Hard merging is off.
A deterministic 26-connected nearest-instance watershed then fills empty
Stage-1 anatomy-mask voxels from existing Stage-2 markers. It preserves Stage-2
support and other anatomies, and creates no marker-free object. All weights
remain v3.5. On a frozen 68-patient/132-anatomy local proxy, Dice/HD95/ASSD
changed from v3.6.2 0.8627/6.359/1.766 mm to 0.8656/6.262/1.721 mm; Merge
20 to 19 and Split 337 to 340. These are local proxy, not hidden-test, values.
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Job requires gpu type]
----------------------------------------------------------------
NVIDIA T4 Tensor Core GPU (16 GiB VRAM)

Stage A and each anatomy expert are loaded and released serially. Guarded-seed
refinement and Stage-1 fill run deterministically on CPU.
