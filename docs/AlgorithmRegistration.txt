Anatomy-Routed Split-Aware RF STU-Net (PENGWIN-Auto)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Description] (max 1024 chars)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Pelvic and femoral fracture-fragment instance segmentation in CT. A serial
two-stage STU-Net-B cascade predicts anatomy, routes pelvic/femur cases with a
validated hybrid random forest, and applies a Sacrum, shared-Hip, or Femur
expert producing four ABBC and nine 1/3/9-voxel affinity channels. The exact
v3.5 one-voxel partition remains the safe base. A parallel 1/3/9 RAG-veto plus
full-ABBC path proposes local binary splits. Five candidate-outcome RF
regressors use 44 inference-only features to predict changes in Merge, Split,
Dice, F1, and Precision; only candidates passing a conservative split-cost
policy are applied. Foreground support is unchanged. Neural weights and the
family router remain v3.5; the new model archive adds the RF bundle. Nested
case-grouped OOF on 68 patients/132 anatomies changed Dice 0.8556 to 0.8561,
HD95 6.687 to 6.440 mm, ASSD 1.852 to 1.789 mm, Merge 21 to 20, and Split 330
to 334. These are local proxy values, not hidden-test scores.
----------------------------------------------------------------
