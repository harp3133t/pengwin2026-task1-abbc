Anatomy-Routed Progressive Affinity-ABBC STU-Net (PENGWIN-Auto)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Description] (max 1024 chars)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Pelvic and femoral fracture-fragment instance segmentation in CT. A serial
two-stage STU-Net-B cascade first predicts anatomy and uses a validated hybrid
random-forest router to select pelvic or femur. The routed Sacrum, shared-Hip
or Femur expert emits four ABBC and nine 1/3/9-voxel affinity channels. The A1
decoder builds an affinity RAG-veto partition, applies full ABBC split/merge
refinement, and requires agreement from all three affinity ranges for predicted
1-5 cm3 merge candidates (two of three for larger candidates). Stage A, router,
ROI policy, experts and weights are unchanged from v3.5. On a frozen
68-patient/132-anatomy official-aligned local proxy, A1 reached Dice 0.8853,
HD95 3.656 mm, ASSD 0.901 mm, recall 0.9462, precision 0.8854 and instance F1
0.8979. Small-fragment recall was 34/53 and large-fragment recall 262/267.
